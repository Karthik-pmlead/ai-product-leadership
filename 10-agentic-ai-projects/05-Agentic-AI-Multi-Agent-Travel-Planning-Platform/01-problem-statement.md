# Problem Statement

## Context

Planning a multi-day trip requires travelers to coordinate flights, accommodation, and destination activities across multiple information sources.

These decisions are interdependent:
- Flight schedules influence hotel check-in and check-out.
- Hotel location affects activity accessibility and travel time.
- Budget and travel preferences influence choices across all domains.
- Activities must fit within the available travel schedule.

Today, travelers often research these independently and manually reconcile the results.

## Problem

Fragmented travel research creates a disconnected planning experience, increasing user effort and making it difficult to produce a coherent, personalized itinerary.

Traditional search experiences provide individual options but leave the responsibility of coordinating and validating the overall trip with the traveler.

## Product Opportunity

Build an AI-powered travel planning platform that accepts high-level trip requirements and coordinates specialized agents to research, consolidate, and validate travel options.

## Target User

Travelers planning multi-day trips who want personalized recommendations without manually researching and coordinating every travel component.

## Desired Outcome

A consolidated itinerary that aligns flight options, accommodation, activities, budget, travel dates, and user preferences.

**Product principle:** Optimize for a coherent end-to-end travel experience, not isolated recommendations.
