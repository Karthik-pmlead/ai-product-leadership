# Success Metrics

## Objective

Evaluate whether the Product Launch Command Center reduces repetitive stakeholder coordination while providing relevant and reliable responses.

The following are **proposed evaluation metrics**, not measured production outcomes.

| Metric | What It Measures |
|---|---|
| Intent Routing Accuracy | Percentage of questions routed to the correct specialist. |
| Response Relevance | Whether responses address the stakeholder's actual question. |
| Knowledge Accuracy | Whether answers are consistent with assigned launch knowledge. |
| Response Time | Time taken to provide a useful response. |
| Task Completion Rate | Percentage of stakeholder queries resolved without PM/TPM intervention. |
| Cross-Domain Leakage | Frequency of irrelevant information appearing in specialist responses. |
| Voice Interaction Success | Percentage of voice interactions completed successfully. |

## Evaluation Approach

Test representative stakeholder questions across technical, marketing, and executive domains.

Evaluate:
- Correct specialist selection.
- Accuracy and relevance of responses.
- Appropriate knowledge usage.
- Follow-up conversation handling.
- Separation of specialist knowledge domains.

## Desired Outcome

Enable stakeholders to access launch information independently, reduce repetitive PM/TPM communication, and improve the speed and consistency of launch-related information sharing.
