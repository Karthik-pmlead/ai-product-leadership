# Problem Statement

## Problem

During a product launch, Product Managers and Technical Program Managers become the central communication point for multiple teams.

They repeatedly answer questions from:

- **Engineering:** Technical readiness, deployment, QA, and bugs.
- **Marketing:** Campaign plans, press timelines, and messaging.
- **Sales:** Competitive positioning and customer objections.
- **Executives:** Launch status, risks, budget, and timelines.

This creates a coordination bottleneck. PMs and TPMs spend valuable time answering recurring questions instead of focusing on decisions and execution.

## Opportunity

Create a multimodal AI Product Launch Command Center that allows stakeholders to independently access relevant launch information through voice or text.

## Product Goal

Reduce repetitive stakeholder coordination through specialized AI agents while keeping PMs and TPMs focused on launch outcomes.

## Target Users

- Product Managers
- Technical Program Managers
- Engineering and QA Leaders
- Marketing and Sales Teams
- Executive Stakeholders
