# Knowledge Scoping Strategy

## Objective

Give each specialist access to the information needed for its responsibilities while minimizing irrelevant cross-domain information.

## Knowledge Allocation

| Agent | Knowledge Access |
|---|---|
| Technical Readiness Specialist | Technical launch knowledge |
| GTM & Marketing Specialist | Marketing and sales knowledge |
| Executive Briefing Specialist | Consolidated launch knowledge across domains |

## Example

A technical specialist should answer questions about deployment readiness, infrastructure, and API latency—not paid social advertising spend.

Similarly, the marketing specialist should focus on campaigns, press timelines, and competitive messaging—not p99 API latency.

The Executive Briefing Specialist needs visibility across all domains to answer broader leadership questions.

## Design Principles

1. **Relevance:** Give specialists information aligned with their responsibilities.
2. **Separation:** Keep technical and marketing knowledge distinct.
3. **Synthesis:** Provide consolidated knowledge to the executive specialist.
4. **Consistency:** Ground responses in the designated launch information.

## Expected Benefit

Knowledge scoping improves response relevance, reduces unrelated information in specialist answers, and supports cross-functional executive summaries.

*Note: Knowledge scoping is an architectural control. It does not, by itself, guarantee zero information leakage or prevent incorrect model responses.*
