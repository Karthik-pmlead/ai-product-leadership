# Problem Statement

## Problem
NovaCart leadership relies on internal documents and operational information to answer business questions. Manually searching across knowledge sources slows decision-making and can produce inconsistent answers.

## Opportunity
Build a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) solution that enables users to ask natural-language questions and receive answers grounded in available internal knowledge.

## Target Users
- Business leaders
- Product and operations teams
- Internal knowledge users

## Example Questions
- What was NovaPhone X1 revenue in Q4?
- How many NovaHome SmartHub Pro units were sold last year?
- What is the churn rate for NovaCart Essentials Subscription?

## Product Outcome
Enable faster, more reliable access to enterprise knowledge while reducing unsupported AI-generated answers.
