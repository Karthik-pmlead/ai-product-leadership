# Product Requirements

## Functional Requirements
1. Ingest documents from Google Drive.
2. Split documents into chunks and generate embeddings.
3. Store embeddings in Pinecone for retrieval.
4. Accept natural-language questions through a chat interface.
5. Retrieve relevant content and rerank results.
6. Generate answers using retrieved information.
7. Abstain when relevant information is unavailable.

## Non-Functional Requirements
- Answer grounding and retrieval relevance.
- Secure credential and data handling.
- Measurable response quality and latency.

## Future Enhancements
- Source citations and traceability.
- Structured business-metric analysis.
- Anomaly explanations and email reports.
