# Monthly Financial Standing Report Structure

## Report Header
- Report title: NovaCart Monthly Financial Standing Report
- Reporting month and year
- Report generation date

## 1. Executive Summary
- Overall business performance
- Key financial observations
- Significant market developments
- Major risks and opportunities

## 2. KPI Dashboard
Present relevant retrieved business and financial KPIs.

| KPI | Current Value | Context / Observation | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Relevant KPI | Retrieved value | Evidence-based observation | Source reference |

## 3. Performance Deep Dive
- Revenue and sales performance
- Product performance
- Relevant business trends
- Significant changes and observations

## 4. Market Context
- Industry developments
- Consumer behavior trends
- Relevant market intelligence
- Potential business implications

## 5. Drivers & Correlations
- Internal performance trends
- Relevant external market factors
- Potential relationships between observed developments
- Supporting evidence

## 6. Risks, Opportunities & Recommendations

| Category | Observation | Supporting Evidence | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Risk | Identified risk | Retrieved evidence | Suggested action |
| Opportunity | Identified opportunity | Retrieved evidence | Suggested action |

## 7. Sources
List the retrieved internal and external sources used in the analysis.

Clearly distinguish internal business information from external market intelligence.

## Reporting Guidelines
- Use concise executive-level language.
- Present financial figures accurately.
- Avoid unsupported conclusions.
- Identify unavailable information.
- Use readable HTML tables and headings.
- Ensure recommendations are grounded in the available evidence.
