# Success Metrics

## Objective
Evaluate the effectiveness of the automated monthly financial reporting workflow.

| Dimension | Success Metric | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Automation | Monthly report generation | Report generated through scheduled workflow |
| Timeliness | Report delivery | Email delivered within the monthly reporting cycle |
| Coverage | Knowledge-source utilization | Internal and external sources queried |
| Completeness | Report structure | All required report sections generated |
| Accuracy | Evidence-grounded findings | Claims supported by retrieved source information |
| Reliability | Workflow execution | Successful completion of ingestion, retrieval, generation, and delivery |

## Measurement Approach

Validate the workflow using representative internal and external documents. Review generated reports for source coverage, completeness, factual accuracy, and successful email delivery.

*Note: Quantitative performance targets and measured business outcomes will be established through evaluation and operational usage.*
