# Problem Statement

## Background
NovaCart's Product Finance team prepares monthly financial performance reports by combining internal business data with external market intelligence.

## Problem
The reporting process involves manually gathering information from multiple sources, reviewing performance metrics, researching market developments, and consolidating findings for leadership.

This creates repetitive effort, increases the possibility of inconsistent analysis, and makes timely reporting more challenging.

## Product Opportunity
Build an AI-powered financial intelligence workflow that retrieves internal performance data and external market information, synthesizes relevant insights, and automatically delivers a structured monthly report.

## Desired Outcome
Provide leadership with a consistent, evidence-based view of NovaCart's financial standing and the market conditions influencing business performance.
